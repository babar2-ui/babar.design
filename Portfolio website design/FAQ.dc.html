✳  FAQ

Answers before you ask.

Everything about process, timelines, and working together. Anything missing? Just ask.

✳What does a typical project with you look like?+

Most engagements run 4–12 weeks: a discovery sprint, then weekly design cycles with reviews, ending in a documented handoff. You get progress you can see every week.

✳How long does a redesign usually take?+

A focused feature redesign takes 3–4 weeks; a full product redesign typically 8–12, depending on research depth and the number of platforms.

✳Do you work with existing design systems and codebases?+

Yes — I audit what exists first and extend it rather than replace it. I'm comfortable working directly with engineers in Figma, tokens, and component code.

✳Can you join our team full-time or embedded?+

Yes. I'm open to full-time roles and embedded contract work, remote-first, across time zones.

✳What do you charge?+

Fixed project pricing after a short scoping call, or a weekly rate for embedded work. Reach out with your goals and I'll send a proposal within 48 hours.

✳Which tools do you work in?+

Figma for design and systems, Framer and Protopie for prototyping, Maze and Dovetail for research — and whatever your team already uses for tickets and docs.

✳How do we start?+

Send a short note through the contact form with what you're building and what's slowing you down. I'll reply within a day with next steps.
