About Muhammad Babar — UI UX Designer for AI & SaaS Products
✳  ABOUT ME

UI UX designer helping AI and SaaS products grow through better design.

I'm Muhammad Babar, a UI UX designer with 7+ years of experience turning complex ideas into simple, scalable digital experiences that drive business growth.

✳  MY STORY

I started in graphic design and found my home in product design — where craft meets evidence.

Over the past seven years I've designed digital products for startups and enterprise teams across fintech, healthcare, SaaS, e-commerce, and education. Early in my career I learned that a beautiful interface fails if it doesn't solve the right problem, so I built my process around research, testing, and measurable outcomes.

Today I focus on AI product design and SaaS platforms: products with real complexity, where clear UX decides whether users trust the system or abandon it.

No Code Design

✳  HOW I THINK

Design is a business tool. Every screen should move a metric that matters.
