Problem& Solution

A trading workspace brings together dense, fast-moving information. The design challenge is to help traders read the market, configure an order and stay aware of their account.

!The challenge

Price action, instrument lists, execution controls and challenge rules all compete for attention. A useful terminal needs a stable hierarchy that keeps these decisions connected.

01Make the market the centre

Give the chart the primary canvas. Keep the market list to the side and positions below, so analysis remains the anchor of the workspace.

02Bring context to the action

Group volume, margin, costs and protective settings within the order panel. Keep challenge targets and loss limits visible in a dedicated account area.

DesignInsights

Three observations connect the individual screens into a coherent design direction. Each follows a visible choice in the supplied interface.

01

Hierarchy gives attention a home.

A dominant chart, a dedicated market rail and a position area below establish a recognisable structure for the session.

02

Context belongs beside control.

Order sizing, cost information, margin and protective settings are most useful when they share the same decision space.

One decision.
Full context.
03

Progress needs a clear signal.

Values, progress bars and explicit status labels work together to make different challenge rules easier to interpret.
