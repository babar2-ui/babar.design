01 THE PROJECT

A world of sport.
One place to belong.

Rally brings players, venues, coaches and communities into one connected mobile experience. From choosing a sport to finding a game, the product makes the next step visible.

PRODUCTRally Player App
SECTORSports & recreation
DESIGN SCOPEUX flows · UI · Design system
PLATFORMMobile + venue ecosystem
THE DESIGN CHALLENGE

Getting people to play
starts before the game.

A player needs more than a free court. They need the right time, compatible players, clear booking details and a reason to come back.

01 / FIND

Make discovery useful.

Bring sport, date, time and location into the decision so players can narrow their options.

02 / COMMIT

Make the plan clear.

Show the venue, people, skill levels and price before the player commits to a session.

03 / RETURN

Make it feel social.

Connect the booking journey to conversations, communities and the player’s ongoing progress.
