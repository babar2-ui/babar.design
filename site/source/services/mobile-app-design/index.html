/ OVERVIEW

Mobile is unforgiving. You have one screen, one thumb, and a few seconds to prove the app is worth keeping.

I design iOS and Android apps that respect platform conventions and human attention: clear hierarchy, generous touch targets, honest loading states, and flows that work on a slow connection. From first launch to habit, mobile app design is about removing everything between a user and the thing they came to do. I design for both iOS and Android, respecting each platform's patterns where they matter and keeping one shared design system underneath. Work usually covers onboarding, navigation, the core daily flows, notifications and settings, with every empty, loading and error state designed. You get interactive prototypes to test with real users and a handoff your developers can build from without guessing.

/ THE CHALLENGE

Where mobile apps lose users.

Users churn in the first session

Too much setup before any payoff, so the app never gets a second open.

The app is a shrunken desktop

Dense tables and hover patterns that were never designed for a thumb.

Navigation gets lost

Deep hierarchies with no clear way back to the thing users actually need.

Store ratings tell the story

Reviews describe confusion and crashes rather than the value you built.

/ FAQS
Do you design for both iOS and Android?

Yes. I design platform-appropriate versions that share one design system, respecting Human Interface Guidelines and Material Design where they differ.

Can you work with React Native or Flutter teams?

Yes — I structure components and tokens to map cleanly onto cross-platform frameworks.

How long does a mobile app design take?

A focused MVP with three to four core flows typically takes four to six weeks. Larger apps scale from there.

Do you handle app store assets?

I can — screenshots, listing visuals, and icon variations as an add-on to the core design work.

Do you design for tablets and different screen sizes?

Yes. I design responsive layouts that adapt from small phones to tablets, and I check key flows on large and small screens so navigation, touch targets and content density stay comfortable on every device your users have.
