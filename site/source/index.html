✳Who do you work with?+

Startups, AI and SaaS companies, fintech teams and B2B businesses that need a product that looks premium and is easy to use. Over 8 years and 300+ projects, I’ve designed for clients in the US, UK, GCC and Australia/New Zealand, from founders building an MVP to teams redesigning a product people already use.

✳What does your UI UX design process look like?+

It has four stages: Discover (research and user needs), Define (user flows and wireframes), Design (high-fidelity UI, design system and prototypes) and Deliver (developer handoff and QA support). Each stage ends with a review, so there are no surprises at the end.

✳Do you design in Figma, and will I own the files?+

Yes to both. Figma is my primary tool, and every file is built with auto layout, components and design tokens so any designer or developer can pick it up after me. Once the project is paid in full, you own everything.

✳Do you use AI in your design process?+

Yes, as a tool, not a replacement for judgment. I use Claude, Anthropic’s AI model, to speed up research, UX writing, design system documentation and prototyping. That removes slow, repetitive work, so more of your budget goes into the decisions that actually move your product forward.

✳Can you design and build my website, not just design it?+

Yes. With Claude-assisted design and implementation, I turn Figma designs into working front-end builds and interactive prototypes you can click through in a browser. For marketing sites and landing pages, I deliver both design and code. For complex web apps, I work alongside your developers.

✳You’re based in Lahore. How do you handle time zones?+

Most of my clients are overseas, so remote work is how I work every day. I overlap working hours with each client, run weekly calls, and share progress asynchronously through Figma and Loom. You’ll always know where your project stands.

✳How much does a project cost?+

It depends on scope and complexity. Projects typically start from $1,500 for a landing page and $6,000 for a full product design. I offer fixed-price projects and monthly retainers, and you’ll get a clear quote after a free discovery call.

✳How do we get started?+

Book a short discovery call. We’ll talk through your product, users and goals, and within 48 hours you’ll get a proposal with scope, timeline and cost.
